The catalog seed and every example prompt are now US Forest Service only. The
organization seed lists just the Forest Service and its 141 national forests
and grasslands; BLM, DOT, state wildlife, NPS and TVA and their offices are no
longer seeded. The seed only adds and updates rows, so a database seeded
before this keeps those organizations until they are archived by hand.

The entry page drops the five examples a business applies for (grazing,
outfitter, ski area, power line and mineral exploration permits) and keeps 16
Forest Service forestry, restoration and facilities projects. The new-project
dialog's BLM road repair suggestion becomes a Tahoe NF one. The signup loading
screen swaps its BLM and Yellowstone facts for Forest Service ones. The landing
page's hero examples (pipelines, solar, highways, dredging, airports, housing)
are replaced with 14 national forest projects.
