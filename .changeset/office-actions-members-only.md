Office pages only offer management actions the viewer is allowed to use:
"Manage Office" needs update rights on the office, "New Project" and the
empty state's "Create First Project" need create rights, and "Invite
Members" needs member management. Signed-in non-members browsing a
government office no longer see buttons the API would reject. Permissions
are resolved on the server in the office layout, so nothing flashes in.
