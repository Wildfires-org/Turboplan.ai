Mobile layout: phones no longer get a permanent icon rail eating a sixth of
the screen. A menu button in the page header opens the full sidebar as a
sheet, which closes on navigation and shows a close button instead of the
desktop-only pin. The compact sticky banner keeps just the title on phones,
and the tabs/search/filter band only sticks from tablet width up, so
scrolled content gets most of the screen.
