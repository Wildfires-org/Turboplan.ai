Redesign the landing page in a glass ("Mist") style and add FAQ and contact
sections to the homepage.

- Homepage: floating glass navbar, inset prompt input, glass quick-start pills,
  the interactive product showcase directly under the hero, a looping partner
  logo band, glass feature and pricing cards, a new FAQ accordion, a contact
  section (email with copy button, Enterprise and agency options) and a
  contained call-to-action panel. Green primary buttons use a new gradient style.
- `/contact` now redirects to `/#contact`; the standalone contact page is gone.
- Catalog, organization, office, project and template pages, checkout, 404/error
  pages and `/docs` use the same layout width, type scale and glass cards. Docs
  sidebar and table of contents no longer overlap the navbar or footer, and
  phones get a docs menu and search bar.
- Consistent motion: shared easing, press feedback, shorter entrances, and
  reduced-motion support throughout.
- Contrast fixes for green and grey text on glass surfaces.
