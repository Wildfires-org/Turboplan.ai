Emails now carry the Wildfires.org brand only. The header shows the orange
Wildfires.org mark beside the app name, set in Inter instead of a monospace
font (Gmail fell back to Courier). The card's hero band is warm cream with the
mark in place of the previous mascot, the brand eyebrow is forest on sage, and
the CTA uses the forest gradient of the app's Wildfires `btn-primary` (solid
`#1A3B21` fallback for Outlook). Links, borders, the details box and the page
background moved from the old palette to forest, sage and cream; the warning
tone now uses the Wildfires orange scale. The footer reads "TurboPlan · open
source by Wildfires.org".

The app's public email images are replaced: `logo.png` (56px) and
`logo-hero.png` (128px) are new, and the old mascot and icon PNGs are removed.
