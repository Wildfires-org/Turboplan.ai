This guide is for **government agency reviewers** — the staff who receive
projects, rather than the citizens or planners who create them. When someone
submits a project to a government organization for review, this is what happens
on your side.

## The submissions inbox

Submitted projects arrive in a **submissions inbox scoped to your office**. You
see the projects submitted to your office in one place, so reviewing is a matter
of working through the inbox rather than hunting across the app.

## The submission lifecycle

Each submission moves through a simple status lifecycle:

- **Submitted** — the project has been sent to your office and is waiting on a
  decision.
- **Accepted** — you've approved the submission.
- **Rejected** — you've declined the submission.

From a submitted item, you act on it by accepting or rejecting it, which moves
it out of the pending state.

## Notifications

When you accept or reject a submission, an **email notification** is sent, so
the submitter learns the outcome without checking back manually.
