Billing is handled per organization through Stripe subscriptions. An
organization picks a plan, and that plan governs how many projects it can run
and how many seats it includes.

## Plans

There are three plans:

- **Starter** — the entry-level plan.
- **Pro** — a higher tier with more capacity.
- **Max** — the top tier.

Pricing is **seat-based**: each plan includes a per-seat cost, so what you pay
reflects both the plan and the number of members on it.

## The Starter hard limit

**Starter enforces a hard limit.** Once an organization on Starter reaches its
plan limits, creating new projects is blocked until the organization upgrades to
a higher-tier plan. Pro and Max are the higher tiers you upgrade into for more
capacity.

## Knowing you're approaching a limit

The system sends **usage-alert emails** as your organization consumes its plan.
Alerts fire at **70%**, **90%**, and **100%** of plan usage, so you get advance
warning well before hitting the ceiling — and a final notice when you reach it.
If you're on Starter and hit 100%, that's when new project creation stops until
you upgrade.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the system sends **payment-failure emails** so you can
update your billing details and keep the subscription active.

## Managing billing and seats

Billing and seats are managed at the **organization level**. Open your
organization's settings and go to its billing page to review the current plan,
manage seats, and change plans. Because billing is org-scoped, these settings
live with the organization rather than with an individual project.
