Comments give a project a place for discussion — both internal notes among the
team and public engagement with citizens on the catalog.

## Threaded comments

Comments are scoped to a project and support **one level of threading**: people
can reply to a top-level comment, but replies don't nest further. This keeps a
conversation readable as a top-level comment plus its direct replies, rather
than an ever-deepening tree.

## Public and private visibility

Each comment carries a **visibility flag**:

- **Public** — the comment appears on the project's page in the public catalog,
  where anyone browsing can see it.
- **Private** — the comment stays internal and is only visible to people working
  on the project.

Use private comments for internal team discussion, and public comments for
anything meant to be part of the project's public record.

## AI auto-responses

On public catalog project pages, comments can receive an **automatic AI
response**. When a citizen leaves a comment, the system can post an automated
reply under a configurable responder name — for example, an acknowledgment on
behalf of the government office. This lets citizen comments get a timely
response without a staff member replying to each one by hand.
