TurboPlan handles project documents and their signatures through two related
modules: **Documents** and **Signing**.

## Documents

The **Documents** module lets you upload, preview, and manage a project's PDF
and Word files. Documents live inside the project, so everyone with access sees
the same set of files.

## Requesting signatures

The **Signing** module lets you request and complete document signatures. To
collect signatures, you request them on a document and choose the recipients.
Recipients can be arranged as:

- **Parallel** — everyone can sign at the same time.
- **Sequential** — recipients sign in order, one after another.

Recipients sign through an embedded view, and once signing is complete the
signed PDF is stored.

## Tracking pending signatures

Signature requests can span many projects, so there's a dedicated **My
Signatures** page that tracks pending signatures across all of your projects in
one place. Check it to see what's waiting on you or on others, without opening
each project individually.
