The <a href={routing.catalog()}>public catalog</a> — the **Projects** entry in
the site menu — is a browsable, no-account view of what government
organizations are working on and the templates they've published. Anyone can
look — signing in only adds the ability to build on what you find.

## Browsing the catalog

The catalog is organized as a multi-level browse that mirrors the app's
hierarchy:

- **Organizations** — the government organizations publishing to the catalog.
- **Offices** — the offices within an organization.
- **Projects and templates** — the published projects and templates under an
  office.
- **Template detail** — the full view of a single template.

You can drill down from an organization to its offices to their projects and
templates, all without an account.

## Creating a project from a template

When you're **signed in**, a **Create from template** button appears on a
template. Using it creates a new project in your own workspace from that
template and drops you into it in the main app. If you're logged out, the button
doesn't appear — you'll be prompted to sign in or sign up first.

## Publishing to the catalog

The catalog's contents come from the organization side: **government
organizations can publish projects and templates** into the catalog to make them
publicly visible. That's how the organizations, offices, and templates you
browse get there in the first place. As a visitor or signed-in user, you're on
the receiving end — browsing what's been published and building on it.
