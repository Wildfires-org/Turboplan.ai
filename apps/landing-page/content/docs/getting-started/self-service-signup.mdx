If you don't have an invite to an existing organization yet, you don't need one.
TurboPlan has a **self-service** path that lets a new visitor sign up and start
creating a project on their own.

## Signing up on your own

The self-service flow is a distinct starting point from being invited into an
existing organization. You sign up and go **straight into creating your first
project** — there's no waiting to be added to a government organization first.

As part of this flow, the app **automatically creates your personal
organization and workspace**, so your new project has a home from the moment you
start. It's the same personal-workspace mechanism every account gets; you're
simply entering it from the self-service door rather than from an invite.

From there, you land in the project's chat and set it up like any other project.
